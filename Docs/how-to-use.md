# How to Use Mythos Log

Mythos Log turns your real habits into skills you can build over time. Set a weekly baseline, log what you do, and see how each completed week affects your progress and rank.

## Getting Started

The first time you open the app, follow the setup screens:

1. Choose the starter habits you want to track. All seven core skills are included, and you can change habits later.
2. Set a **baseline** for each skill: the amount you normally do in a week. Be honest about your current routine; this sets your starting rank.
3. Optionally add a **target** and **personal max** for each skill. You can skip these and add them later.
4. Tap **Begin Training**.

The **Dashboard** shows your skills and this week's progress. Use the tabs at the bottom to open **Review**, **Goals**, and **More**.

## Logging Activity

1. On the **Dashboard**, tap the log button on a skill card. You can also tap **Log** beside a skill in **Review**.
2. If the skill has several habits, choose the one you did.
3. Enter the amount, such as minutes, pages, or a session. Change the date if you're logging something from earlier, and add a note if you like.
4. Tap **Save Log** or **Complete Session**.

Tap a skill card to see its habits and weekly total. Open a habit to use its quick log buttons, make a custom log, or see recent entries. To remove an entry logged by mistake, swipe left on it in the habit's **Recent Logs** list and tap **Delete**.

## Skills, Habits, and Baselines

A **skill** is an area you want to improve, such as Strength or Reading. A **habit** is a specific activity you log under that skill. Habits linked to the same skill contribute to its weekly total.

Your **baseline** is your normal weekly expectation. It drives the progress shown on the Dashboard and your rank changes. A habit can also have its own daily or weekly target to help you track that activity.

- To add a habit, open its skill on the **Dashboard** and tap **Add** in the **Habits** section.
- To change a habit, open it and tap **Edit**. You can change its name, measurement, schedule, target, or whether it is active.
- To change a skill's baseline or measurement unit, open the skill and tap **Recalibrate**.
- To enable, archive, or reorder skills, go to **More → Manage Skills**. Archiving keeps the skill and its history.

## Charge and Ranks

At the end of each week, Mythos Log compares each skill's logged total with its baseline. Going above your baseline can earn **Charge** toward the next rank. Meeting your baseline maintains your progress. Falling short can reduce Charge or build up a deficit; repeated shortfalls may lower a rank, depending on your progression settings.

Rank changes happen when the week is finalized. You can adjust strictness, regression, and decay under **More → Settings → Progression**.

## Weekly Review and History

The **Review** tab shows which skills are on pace, behind, or at risk this week. Tap a skill's name for its details, or use **Log** to add activity. Viewing Review does not finalize the week or change your data.

With the default Monday week start, the previous week finalizes automatically after Sunday at midnight. Open **Review** for the result and a breakdown by skill. Go to **More → History** to see trends and earlier weeks. You can change the week start in **More → Settings → Progression**.

## Goals

Goals give you a separate target to work toward over a chosen period. They do not replace your weekly skill baseline.

1. Open **Goals** and tap **+** or **Create your first goal**.
2. Choose what the goal tracks and set a target. You can link it to a skill or a particular habit.
3. Add an end date and priority if you want, then save it.

Tap a goal to edit it. Goals are for tracking by default. If you want meeting a goal to earn bonus Charge, enable **Goals affect charge** in **More → Settings → Calibration & Goals**, then turn on **Goal affects progression** for that goal.

## Apple Health, Shortcuts, and Widgets

To import workouts, go to **More → Settings → Apple Health**, tap **Connect Apple Health**, and allow access. Turn on **Auto-import workouts** for ongoing imports, or tap **Sync Now**. Under **Workout Types**, choose which activities to import. If an imported workout cannot be matched to a habit, open the prompt on the relevant skill to assign it, create a matching habit, or ignore it.

You can also use supported Mythos Log actions in Apple's **Shortcuts** app to log common activities or open a skill. Add a Mythos Log widget from your device's widget gallery for a quick view of your progress.

## Sync, Backups, and Reminders

Your data is saved on your device. When iCloud sync is available, it can also sync across devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Check its status under **More → Settings → Data**. The same section has **Export JSON** and **Import JSON** for a copy of your data.

For daily, evening, weekly, or at-risk reminders, open **More → Settings → Notifications**, turn on the reminders you want, and allow notification access when prompted.

## Troubleshooting

**My activity is missing from this week's total.** Check the log's date and that the habit belongs to the skill you're viewing. If it came from Apple Health, check for an unmatched workout on that skill.

**My workout is not importing.** Check Apple Health access, **Auto-import workouts**, and the selected **Workout Types** in Settings. Tap **Sync Now** to try a manual import.

**My data is not showing on another device.** Check **More → Settings → Data** on both devices and make sure they use the same Apple Account. iCloud sync can take time to complete.

**I'm not receiving reminders.** Check that the reminder is enabled in Mythos Log and that notifications are allowed for the app in your device's settings.
